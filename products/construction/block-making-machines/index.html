Block Making Machines

Building stronger communities, one block at a time.

2-Year Warranty Genuine Spare Parts Technical Support Dealer Network Fast Delivery

Burgenton Block Making Machines are engineered to produce high-quality concrete blocks efficiently, consistently, and cost-effectively.

Designed for entrepreneurs, contractors, real estate developers, and block manufacturers, our machines help increase production capacity while maintaining the strength and uniformity required for modern construction projects.

Whether supplying blocks for housing developments, commercial buildings, infrastructure projects, or local markets, Burgenton provides reliable equipment solutions that support business growth and quality construction.
