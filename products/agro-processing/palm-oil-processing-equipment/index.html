Agro-Processing Equipment — Model BURGENTON BPO-1000pro

Palm Oil Processing Line

Designed to maximize extraction efficiency and improve production output.

2-Year Warranty Genuine Spare Parts Technical Support Dealer Network Fast Delivery

The Burgenton BPO-1000pro Palm Oil Processing Line is configured for small commercial crude palm oil production from fresh fruit bunches. It combines the principal extraction and oil-cleaning stages in a coordinated semi-mechanised line.

The arrangement is suitable for farmer cooperatives, community processing centres and SMEs seeking improved recovery, cleaner handling and more consistent product quality than traditional processing methods.
