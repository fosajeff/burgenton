Agro-Processing Equipment — Model BURGENTON PBL-500pro

Peanut Butter Processing Line

Converting roasted peanuts into smooth, consistent, ready-to-package peanut butter.

2-Year Warranty Genuine Spare Parts Technical Support Dealer Network Fast Delivery

The Burgenton PBL-500pro is an integrated commercial peanut butter processing solution designed to convert roasted peanuts into smooth, consistent and ready-to-package peanut butter. The line combines continuous roasting, cooling, peeling, inspection, two-stage grinding, ingredient mixing, controlled cooling, vacuum degassing and semi-automatic filling.

Its modular configuration allows the production process to be adapted for smooth, fine or textured peanut butter. Stainless-steel product-contact components, adjustable processing controls and integrated material-handling systems support hygienic operation, consistent product quality and reduced manual handling.
